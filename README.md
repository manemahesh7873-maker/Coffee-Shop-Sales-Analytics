# Coffee Shop Sales Analytics

## Project Overview
Coffee Shop Sales Analytics is an Excel-based business intelligence project designed to analyze coffee shop transaction data and identify sales trends, product performance, store performance, and customer purchasing patterns.

The project transforms raw transaction data into a professional Excel dashboard with interactive analysis and key business KPIs.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall coffee shop sales performance
- Identify top-performing product categories and products
- Compare sales across store locations
- Analyze monthly and daily sales trends
- Understand quantity sold and average unit price
- Identify business trends that can support better decision-making

## Dataset
- **Records:** 149,116 transactions
- **Date Range:** January 1, 2023 – June 30, 2023
- **Store Locations:** 3
- **Product Categories:** 9
- **Product Types:** 29

## Key KPIs
- **Total Sales:** $698,812.33
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 214,470
- **Average Unit Price:** $3.38
- **Product Categories:** 9
- **Product Types:** 29
- **Store Locations:** 3

## Dashboard Analysis
The dashboard provides analysis of:
- Total Sales
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Unit Price
- Sales by Store Location
- Sales by Product Category
- Sales by Product Type
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Daily Sales Trend
- Product and category performance

## Key Business Insights
- Sales performance can be compared across the three store locations to identify stronger and weaker locations.
- Product-level analysis helps identify high-performing products and categories.
- Monthly and daily trends provide visibility into changing sales patterns.
- Quantity and sales analysis can support inventory planning and product-level decision-making.
